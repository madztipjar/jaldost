💧 JalDost — Clean Water Safety Agent

An agentic assistant for ASHA workers, ANMs and Village Water & Sanitation Committees.

Enter field-test-kit (FTK) readings → the agent checks them against BIS IS 10500:2012, explains the risk, plans the fix, and drafts a bilingual report. 100% offline, no data leaves the phone.

Why this matters

600 millionpeople in India face high-to-extreme water stress (NITI Aayog)
~200,000deaths every year from inadequate access to safe water (NITI Aayog)
387 districtshave parts where groundwater nitrate exceeds safe limits (CGWB)
276 districtshave parts with excess fluoride in groundwater (CGWB)

Village FTK testing already happens under the Jal Jeevan Mission — but a raw reading means nothing to a health worker without the standards, the health meaning, and the next action. JalDost's agent supplies all three in under a minute.
